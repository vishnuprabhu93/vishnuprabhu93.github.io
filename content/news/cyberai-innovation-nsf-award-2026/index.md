title: "Dr. Prabhu Awarded NSF CyberAICorps Grant for AI-Driven Cybersecurity Workforce Training"
date: 2026-08-01
category: "Award"
---

Dr. Prabhu has received a three-year, $500,000 grant from the National Science Foundation's
CyberAICorps Scholarship for Service (SFS) Innovation Track to develop an AI-powered training system
for cybersecurity professionals. The project, *CyberAI Innovation: Training the Human Firewall Through
Generative AI Adversaries and Metacognitive Scaffolding for Cybersecurity Workforce Resilience*,
addresses a growing gap between the sophistication of AI-driven cyberattacks and the cognitive
readiness of the professionals defending against them.

Dr. Prabhu serves as Principal Investigator, alongside Co-Principal Investigators Dr. Roger Azevedo
and Dr. Grace Fox. The team will build a multi-agent adaptive tutoring system: an Adversary Agent that
generates realistic AI-enabled threat scenarios (social engineering, prompt injection, and
insider-threat behavior), a Diagnostic Agent that identifies individual gaps in a learner's
self-assessed confidence versus actual performance, and a Metacognitive Scaffolding Agent that
delivers personalized training to close those gaps. Over three years, the system will be evaluated
with 120–200 graduate learners at UCF, a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cybersecurity,
with a pilot at a partner institution coordinated through the Florida High Tech Corridor. All training
materials and system code will be released as open educational resources for adoption at other
institutions.
